Privacy and message handling

tmpmail provides temporary inboxes for development and testing. Anyone who knows an inbox address can read its messages. Please do not use it for private, sensitive, or important information.

Your messages

To receive and display email, we store the message and its attachments, along with the sender, recipient, subject, email headers, and the time it arrived. Inboxes do not require an account or password.

Messages are automatically deleted after one hour by default. The person running the service can change this period, and storage limits may cause messages to be deleted sooner.

tmpmail does not send email on your behalf. It also prevents images hosted on other websites from loading inside messages.

Service activity and analytics

When analytics is enabled, the service keeps a separate record of activity to help its operators understand usage and investigate slow requests, delivery problems, and unwanted traffic. These records may include:

  • Email activity: sender and recipient addresses, the sender’s domain, the sending server’s IP address, delivery time, message size, and how long delivery took. Rejected email attempts may include the addresses involved, the reason code, and the step where the attempt failed.
  • Website and app activity: your connection’s IP address, information your browser or app sends about itself, the type of request, the inbox address or message identifier involved, the time, whether the request succeeded, and how long it took. Automatic inbox checks may be marked separately.

Analytics records do not include message subjects, message contents, or attachments. They can still contain identifying information, such as email addresses and IP addresses; they are not anonymous.

Analytics history is kept for up to 30 days. Operators can choose a shorter period, and storage limits may remove records sooner. Deleting or expiring an email does not immediately delete its separate activity record.

Settings on your device

Your browser remembers the last inbox you opened and your light or dark theme preference. The saved preferences themselves are not collected as analytics. Opening an inbox can still create the activity records described above.

Logs and hosting providers

The service also keeps operational logs to help troubleshoot problems. Depending on its settings, these may include email addresses, IP addresses, request details, and browser or app information, but not message contents. Log storage and retention are managed by the operator and are separate from the analytics history above.

tmpmail does not include advertising or third-party analytics trackers. The hosting provider and services that handle incoming traffic may keep their own logs under their own privacy policies.
